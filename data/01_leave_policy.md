# Acme Robotics — Leave & Time-Off Policy

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees at Acme Robotics accrue 20 days of paid annual leave per year, accruing at a rate of 1.67 days per completed month of service. Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the following calendar year. Any days beyond 5 are forfeited on December 31.

## Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not carry over and is reset on January 1. For any sick absence longer than 3 consecutive working days, a medical certificate must be submitted to the People Operations team within 7 days of returning to work.

## Parental Leave
Acme Robotics offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks for the secondary caregiver. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption of a child. Employees must give at least 8 weeks written notice before commencing parental leave.

## Unpaid Leave
Employees who have exhausted their paid leave may request unpaid leave of up to 30 days per year, subject to manager approval. Unpaid leave does not affect continuity of service but does pause annual leave accrual for the duration.
