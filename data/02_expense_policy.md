# Acme Robotics — Expense & Reimbursement Policy

## Submission Deadlines
All expense claims must be submitted through the Concord portal within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 30 days require director-level approval and may be rejected. Claims older than 90 days will not be reimbursed under any circumstances.

## Travel
Economy class is the standard for all flights under 6 hours. Flights longer than 6 hours may be booked in premium economy with prior manager approval. Business class requires VP approval and is reserved for flights exceeding 9 hours. The daily meal allowance while traveling is $75 in North America and €70 in Europe.

## Accommodation
Hotel bookings must not exceed $250 per night in standard cities and $350 per night in high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo, Zurich). Employees are encouraged to use the preferred booking platform TravelHub to receive corporate rates.

## Equipment
Employees may expense a home-office setup of up to $1,000 in their first year, covering a desk, chair, and monitor. Laptops and phones are provided by the IT department and must not be expensed personally.

## Non-Reimbursable Items
Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, and hotel minibar charges are never reimbursable. Client entertainment involving alcohol requires pre-approval and must be itemized separately.
